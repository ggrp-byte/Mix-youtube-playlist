Privacy Policy — Mix Archive

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Data Collection

Mix Archive does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The extension has no backend server, no analytics, no tracking, and no logging system.

Permissions Used

storage — saves your settings (archive playlist ID, filters, language preference) locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

tabs — detects whether the active tab is a YouTube Mix page so the extension can offer its save functionality. Used only for local page detection.

alarms — used exclusively to poll whether a login flow through ExtensionPay has completed (temporary, max 15 minutes). No data is transmitted.

Host permission (youtube.com) — required to read the current Mix queue and, with your explicit action, write tracks to a playlist you select. All operations happen through your existing YouTube session.

Host permission (extensionpay.com) — optional; used only if you choose to sign up for a Pro account. ExtensionPay handles payment processing under their own privacy policy.

Data Shared with Third Parties

Mix Archive does not sell, share, or transmit user data to any third party. The only external communication is:

  • youtube.com — playlist creation and video addition, performed through your browser session using your existing YouTube authentication.
  • extensionpay.com — only if you use Pro features; ExtensionPay verifies your subscription status.

Children's Privacy

Mix Archive does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top will be revised. The extension itself will not be affected.

Contact

For questions, open an issue at the extension's GitHub repository or contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
